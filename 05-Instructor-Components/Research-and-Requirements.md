# DARB Instructor Components: Research & Requirements

## 1. Instructor User Research & Objectives
Faculty members and department administrators need a streamlined workflow to publish opportunities (internships, competitions, workshops), manage applicant pools, and track student placements without administrative overhead.

## 2. Core Functional Requirements
* **Opportunity Lifecycle Control:** Ability to create, draft, publish, pause, duplicate, and archive opportunities.
* **Applicant Review & Filtering:** Instant access to student GPA, major, academic year, and attached resumes, with multi-criteria filtering.
* **Status Tracking:** Clear visual distinction for application states (Submitted, Under Review, Shortlisted, Accepted, Rejected).
